Questions

Do you pour the concrete for footings and stem walls?

We do the groundwork: digging footings and stem wall trenches to the lines and depth on your plans, and getting the pad ready. Ask us about concrete on your job; we will tell you straight what we handle and what your concrete contractor handles.

Can I rent the excavator without an operator?

No. Our excavator always comes with our operator. We do not dry hire.

How do I get an estimate?

Call or text the job address and what you are building, or use the estimate form. We walk the site, then give you a price and a start time.

What areas do you serve?

Harrodsburg and Mercer County and about 30 miles around, including Danville, Burgin, Perryville, Lawrenceburg, Versailles, Wilmore, Nicholasville and the south and west side of Lexington.

Who calls 811 before digging?

Kentucky 811 must be called before any digging. We will confirm on your estimate who places the locate ticket for your job.

Do you handle drainage problems?

Yes. We put in French drains and culverts and grade the ground so water runs away from your building. We do not do basement waterproofing or foundation repair.
